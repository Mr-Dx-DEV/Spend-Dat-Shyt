PANDORAS BOX INSTALLATION INSTRUCTIONS

FOR STORY MODE:
1. INSTALLATION > PLACE ANIMATION FILES (.YCD) INTO DLC WHEREVER YOU HAVE YOUR ANIMATIONS IN GTA V FILES. THIS CAN BE AN ADDON OR A COPIED MOD FOLDER THAT HOSTS ANIMATION .YCDS

2. MENYOOSTUFF > FAVOURITEANIMS.XML
*EDIT THE DOCUMENT AND ADD THESE LINES OF CODE*

    <Anim dict="pandora@beefitup_fem" name="pandora" /> 
    <Anim dict="pandora@beefitup_male" name="pandora" />
    <Anim dict="pandora@cupidshuffle" name="pandora" />
    <Anim dict="pandora@thewop" name="pandora" /> 
    <Anim dict="pandora@wobble" name="pandora" /> 
    <Anim dict="pandora@bootybump" name="pandora_fem" />
    <Anim dict="pandora@bootybump" name="pandora_male" />

============================================================================================
