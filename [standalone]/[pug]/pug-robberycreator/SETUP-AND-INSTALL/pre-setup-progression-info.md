# Robbery Progression Overview

This is the intended progression path for the prebuilt robbery setup.

The robberies are meant to start small and slowly unlock bigger jobs. Players should begin with low-level crimes, collect useful items, build progression, and then move into larger robberies.

## Progression Flow

The main progression is:

```text
Petty Crimes
    -> ATM Robbery
    -> Store Robbery
    -> Bank Robbery
        -> Bank Truck Robbery
        -> Paleto Bank Robbery
            -> Humane Labs Robbery
                -> Big Bank Robbery
```

In plain English:

1. Players start with Petty Crimes.
2. Petty Crimes help players get started and can lead into ATM robbery.
3. ATM robbery is an early step before bigger robberies.
4. Store robbery comes after the early petty crime and ATM stage.
5. Store robbery helps move players into bank robbery progression.
6. Bank robbery is the first major robbery tier.
7. After bank robbery, progression branches into Bank Truck and Paleto Bank.
8. Paleto Bank continues the main high-tier chain.
9. Paleto Bank leads into Humane Labs.
10. Humane Labs leads into Big Bank robbery.

## Petty Crimes

Petty Crimes are the starting point.

These are small crimes like parking meters, mailboxes, boxes, and porch package robberies.

They are meant to give players an easy way to begin the robbery progression without jumping straight into banks.

Petty Crimes can give players early money, small rewards, and items that help them move into the next robberies.

## ATM Robbery

ATM Robbery comes after Petty Crimes.

This is still an early robbery, but it is a step up from basic petty crimes.

ATM robbery can require tools or items from earlier progression. It gives players a reason to do smaller crimes first before trying better-paying robberies.

## Store Robbery

Store Robbery comes after the early Petty Crime and ATM stage.

This is where players begin doing more structured robberies with more steps.

Store robbery can include things like registers, locked doors, safes, guards, keypads, passcodes, and reward items.

The goal of store robbery is to push players toward the first real bank robbery tier.

## Bank Robbery

Bank Robbery is the first major robbery tier.

This is where the progression starts opening up into bigger jobs.

Once players are ready for bank robbery, they are no longer just doing small crimes. They are entering the main robbery chain.

Bank robbery then branches into two directions:

- Bank Truck Robbery
- Paleto Bank Robbery

## Bank Truck Robbery Branch

Bank Truck Robbery branches off from Bank Robbery.

This means Bank Truck is a higher step than regular bank robbery, but it is not the direct path to Big Bank.

It is a side branch that gives players another bigger-money robbery option after they have reached the bank robbery tier.

## Paleto Bank Robbery Branch

Paleto Bank also branches off from Bank Robbery.

This is the branch that continues the main high-tier progression.

Paleto is meant to be a bigger step than the earlier bank robberies. It should feel like players are moving deeper into the serious robbery chain.

Paleto then leads into Humane Labs.

## Humane Labs Robbery

Humane Labs comes after Paleto Bank.

This is a high-tier robbery after players have already gone through the bank and Paleto progression.

Humane Labs should feel more advanced, with tougher setup, harder stages, better requirements, and stronger rewards.

Humane Labs is the step before Big Bank.

## Big Bank Robbery

Big Bank is the final major robbery in this progression chain.

Players should reach Big Bank after progressing through:

```text
Petty Crimes -> ATM -> Store Robbery -> Bank Robbery -> Paleto Bank -> Humane Labs -> Big Bank
```

Big Bank is meant to be the endgame robbery setup.

It should be the biggest, hardest, and most rewarding robbery in the prebuilt progression.

## Short Version

The progression is:

```text
Petty Crimes
ATM Robbery
Store Robbery
Bank Robbery
Bank Truck or Paleto Bank
Humane Labs from Paleto
Big Bank from Humane Labs
```

Bank Truck is a branch from Bank Robbery.

Paleto Bank is the branch that continues toward Humane Labs.

Humane Labs continues into Big Bank.

Big Bank is the final robbery in the chain.
