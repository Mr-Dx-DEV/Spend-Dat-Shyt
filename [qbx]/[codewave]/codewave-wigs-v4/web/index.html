What can I do with my collected Wigs?

Wigs collected here can be sold to clients around the city in two ways! The first way is using /sellWigs which will make clients come to you to sell! The second way is to buy a client phone & use that to deliver the Wigs across the city, which usually earns more money!

Why level up?

Higher Payouts

Every level increases the money you earn per sale. A Level 10 Legendary Stylist earns 70% more than a newcomer.

Unlock New Recipes

Higher-tier wigs are locked behind levels. Reaching Level 3, 5, 7 and 9 unlocks premium recipes with better yields and higher sale prices.

Prestige Titles

Climb from Newcomer to Legendary Stylist. Each title is a badge of honour on the streets.
